# Sounds and pictures

The games use short sounds and pictures that others made and shared under
free licences. Thank you!

## Sounds

Short excerpts of recordings from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/),
converted to MP3 for all tablets: mono, faded in and out, loudness
normalised, and for long recordings only the loudest few seconds. The files
are in `muckebox/static/sounds/`. Excerpts of CC BY-SA recordings are shared
under the same licence.

## Pictures

The pictures in `muckebox/static/pictures/` are
[Twemoji](https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji) graphics by Twitter, Inc and
other contributors, licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They are used
unchanged.

## Licence texts and checks

The licence texts are in [`LICENSES/`](../LICENSES), and
[`REUSE.toml`](../REUSE.toml) states the licence of every file.
`muckebox/assets.py` lists the same sources for the parents' page
("Quellen der Klänge und Bilder"); a test keeps the files, this list and
`REUSE.toml` in step.
